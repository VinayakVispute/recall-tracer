Sprout Outbreak Demo Plan

Open the demo on an outbreak that is still under investigation. Upload the case report CDC published, and the graph stitches four companies together through one seed lot code and shows which sick people the first recall never reached.

Why this outbreak

  • It is live. CDC and FDA last updated it on October 1, 2026. Judges can open the government pages while we present.
  • It is our product's story in real life. One contaminated ingredient lot (alfalfa seed lot SAL2-24JB) moved through a seed supplier, a sprout grower and two seed repackers to people in 16 states.
  • The data is already in our graph. The openFDA snapshot loaded into FalkorDB holds the seed supplier's, the grower's and one repacker's recalls. Two of them carry the same lot code.
  • It has a real gap. The seed supplier's own recall reaches 6 of the 16 states with cases. The rest were reached only through retail seed packets recalled weeks later.
  • It replaces Clover Hill as the opener. Clover Hill showed one missing state. This shows a multi-company chain, a lot code, and nine states, which is the problem Recall Tracer solves.

One lot, four companies

Public recall records are filed company by company. Nothing in openFDA says these recalls belong together. A graph does it in one step: extract the lot code from each recall's code text, make the lot a node, and every recall that names it hangs off that node.

Solid green: the recall record itself names lot SAL2-24JB. Dashed blue: the FDA advisory states the link.

// FalkorDB: every recall that names the same lot code, across companies
MATCH (f:Firm)-[:RECALLED]->(r:Recall)-[:NAMES_LOT]->(l:LotCode {code: 'SAL2-24JB'})
RETURN f.name, r.id, r.initiated, r.distribution ORDER BY r.initiated

The gap the graph finds

Lay the 16 states with cases over where each company's product went:

The finding. Nine of the sixteen states with sick people are not on the seed supplier's shipping list. The lot reached them only through two retail seed repackers, whose recalls came 19 and 31 days after the supplier's. Any other sprout growers who used the lot are not named in public records at all; FDA's advisory says at least one more grower received it.

This is the honest version of the product's pitch: public records stop at the company. Inside a company, FSMA 204 lot records let the seed supplier list every customer of SAL2-24JB, and each grower list every store, in one query. That is what Recall Tracer does on our demo company.

The upload moment

CDC does not publish individual case reports; it publishes the states, the case total and the onset window. The demo uploads exactly that, as a CSV a health department or a journalist could build from CDC's page:

outbreak,pathogen,state,onset_from,onset_to,source

OB-SPROUTS-2026-08,"STEC E. coli + Salmonella Agona",IN,2026-05-31,2026-09-07,cdc.gov/ecoli/outbreaks/alfalfa-sprouts-08-26
… one row per state: FL IN IA KS MI MN NH NY NC ND OR PA SC SD WA WI

What happens on screen after the upload, in about two seconds and with no agent call:

  1. Candidate recalls. Recalls of the right hazard (STEC or Salmonella) initiated in or after the onset window, ranked by how many case states their distribution reaches. The four sprout and seed recalls rise to the top.
  2. The lot chain. The graph finds that two of them name the same lot, SAL2-24JB, and draws the four-company chain above.
  3. The coverage map. Case states coloured by which recall reaches them: seed supplier, grower, retail only. The nine retail-only states stand out.
  4. The timeline. First illness May 31 · grower recall Aug 22 · seed lot recall Aug 23 · Todd's Sep 11 · Berlin Sep 23.

The chat agent can then answer follow-ups on the same records, for example "Which companies recalled seed lot SAL2-24JB, and which case states did the supplier's recall miss?", citing recall numbers.

What we can and can't claim

Can say. Two openFDA recalls from different companies name lot SAL2-24JB. The FDA advisory links the grower and a second repacker to the same lot. The seed supplier's distribution list covers 6 of the 16 case states. The dates and counts are as published.

Can't say.

  • That Recall Tracer would have found the source earlier. The recall records we join only exist because the investigation produced them.
  • That a case in a given state came from a given company. CDC publishes states, not case-level purchases.
  • That FDA named International Sprout Holdings. FDA's advisory does not; the openFDA recall record carries the lot code.
  • Hospitalization or death counts other than FDA's: 6 hospitalized, no deaths, as of October 1, 2026.

Say it once in the video: "The outbreak and recalls are real public records. The company in the rest of the demo is a demo company."

Backups

  • The upload control isn't built in time. Show the same result on the real-data page, with the Cypher, and say "uploaded case report" as a voice line only.
  • CDC or FDA updates the numbers before recording. Re-scrape the two pages and the openFDA data the morning of the recording; the script's numbers come from those, not from this page.
  • openFDA is unreachable. The snapshot in data/real already holds everything the opener needs.
  • Clover Hill remains a fallback opener. It is loaded and working today.
