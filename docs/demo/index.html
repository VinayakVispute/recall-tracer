Recall Tracer Demo Script

Three minutes, one continuous story. It opens on a real outbreak still under investigation, where one seed lot reached sick people in states its supplier's recall never named, then shows an agent that finds the source from sick people, follows the lot through the graph, and proves which stores are safe. Every number on screen comes from a FalkorDB query.

Written assuming every lane has merged, including the case-report upload from the sprout outbreak plan. If the upload isn't built in time, use the Clover Hill opening under Backups. Numbers are from the seeded graph (seed 42) and the real fda graph, checked on Oct 18.

The story in one breath

Food recalls fail at the edges of the records. When nobody can say which stores got a contaminated lot, companies recall everything, or worse, miss a store. Recall Tracer is an agent that works the recall on a traceability graph. It starts from sick people or a supplier's notice, follows the lot through blending, rework and deliveries, holds what hasn't shipped, pulls only what it must, and proves every other store is clear. A human confirms every decision, and every step can be replayed.

What not to show

  • Waiting. Cut every pause longer than a second, and never show a spinner.
  • Raw JSON, error stacks or the network tab.
  • The chat page as a main feature. It can appear for 3 seconds at most. The dashboard is the product.
  • Claims we can't back: no "detects outbreaks", no "replaces the FDA", no real company names apart from those in the cited FDA and CDC records, and never "this company caused these cases".
  • The phrase "AI decides". Always say "the graph decides, the coordinator confirms".
